# Math Geometry

Proposed Ingagi standard-library modules in this subsystem.

## `std.math`
Constants, elementary functions, and numeric utilities.

## `std.math.scalar`
Scalar operations and numeric predicates.

## `std.math.vector`
2D, 3D, and 4D vectors.

## `std.math.matrix`
Matrices and matrix operations.

## `std.math.quaternion`
Quaternion rotations and interpolation.

## `std.math.transform`
Translation, rotation, scale, and transforms.

## `std.math.complex`
Complex numbers.

## `std.math.dual`
Dual numbers and automatic differentiation support.

## `std.math.interval`
Interval arithmetic.

## `std.math.random`
Random number generators and distributions.

## `std.math.noise`
Perlin, Simplex, and other procedural noise.

## `std.geometry`
Geometric primitives and algorithms.

## `std.geometry.ray`
Rays and ray intersections.

## `std.geometry.plane`
Planes and plane intersections.

## `std.geometry.bounds`
AABB, OBB, bounding spheres, and bounds tests.

## `std.geometry.frustum`
Frustum extraction and culling.

## `std.geometry.intersection`
Intersection and overlap algorithms.

## `std.geometry.triangulation`
Polygon triangulation.

## `std.geometry.spatial`
Spatial partitioning utilities.

## `std.math.curve`
Bézier curves, splines, and interpolation.

## `std.math.color`
Color spaces, conversions, and color operations.
