# Rendering

Proposed Ingagi standard-library modules in this subsystem.

## `std.render`
Rendering abstractions and frame orchestration.

## `std.render.device`
GPU device and capability discovery.

## `std.render.resource`
GPU resources and resource lifetime.

## `std.render.buffer`
Vertex, index, uniform, and storage buffers.

## `std.render.texture`
Textures, formats, views, and samplers.

## `std.render.shader`
Shader modules, compilation, and reflection.

## `std.render.pipeline`
Graphics and compute pipelines.

## `std.render.material`
Materials, parameters, and material instances.

## `std.render.mesh`
Mesh data and GPU mesh resources.

## `std.render.model`
Model instances and renderable assets.

## `std.render.camera`
Camera projections and view state.

## `std.render.lighting`
Light sources and lighting abstractions.

## `std.render.shadow`
Shadow maps and shadow configuration.

## `std.render.postprocess`
Post-processing effects.

## `std.render.pass`
Render passes and render graphs.

## `std.render.graph`
Render-graph resource dependencies and scheduling.

## `std.render.compute`
GPU compute dispatch.

## `std.render.indirect`
Indirect drawing and dispatch.

## `std.render.bindless`
Bindless resource abstractions where supported.

## `std.render.sync`
GPU fences, synchronization, and barriers.

## `std.render.readback`
GPU-to-CPU readback.

## `std.render.debug`
Debug drawing, GPU markers, and profiling integration.
