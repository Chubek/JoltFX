# Audio

Proposed Ingagi standard-library modules in this subsystem.

## `std.audio`
Audio subsystem interfaces.

## `std.audio.device`
Audio devices and output configuration.

## `std.audio.source`
Audio sources and playback.

## `std.audio.buffer`
PCM and decoded audio buffers.

## `std.audio.stream`
Streaming audio.

## `std.audio.spatial`
3D audio positioning.

## `std.audio.listener`
Listener configuration.

## `std.audio.mixer`
Mixing and buses.

## `std.audio.effect`
Audio effects.

## `std.audio.music`
Music playback and transitions.

## `std.audio.spatialize`
Spatialization and attenuation.

## `std.audio.analysis`
Spectrum analysis and metering.
