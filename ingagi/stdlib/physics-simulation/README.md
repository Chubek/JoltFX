# Physics Simulation

Proposed Ingagi standard-library modules in this subsystem.

## `std.physics`
Physics-world interfaces.

## `std.physics.collision`
Collision queries and collision filtering.

## `std.physics.rigidbody`
Rigid bodies and mass properties.

## `std.physics.collider`
Collider shapes and materials.

## `std.physics.constraint`
Joint and constraint abstractions.

## `std.physics.character`
Character-controller interfaces.

## `std.physics.query`
Raycasts, shape casts, and overlap tests.

## `std.physics.vehicle`
Vehicle dynamics abstractions.

## `std.physics.softbody`
Soft-body simulation interfaces.

## `std.physics.fluid`
Fluid simulation interfaces.

## `std.physics.particle`
Particle simulation.

## `std.physics.field`
Force fields and environmental forces.

## `std.simulation`
Fixed-step simulation and deterministic stepping.

## `std.simulation.clock`
Simulation clocks and time-step management.
