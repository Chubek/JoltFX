# Shader

Proposed Ingagi standard-library modules in this subsystem.

## `std.shader`
Shader-language prelude and common declarations.

## `std.shader.math`
Shader-safe math operations.

## `std.shader.vector`
Vector operations.

## `std.shader.matrix`
Matrix operations.

## `std.shader.transform`
Coordinate-space transforms.

## `std.shader.color`
Color conversion and color-space utilities.

## `std.shader.sampling`
Texture sampling helpers.

## `std.shader.lighting`
BRDFs and lighting models.

## `std.shader.pbr`
Physically based rendering utilities.

## `std.shader.shadow`
Shadowing algorithms.

## `std.shader.noise`
Procedural noise.

## `std.shader.derivative`
Derivatives and gradient operations.

## `std.shader.atomic`
Shader atomic operations.

## `std.shader.subgroup`
Subgroup and wave operations where supported.

## `std.shader.encoding`
Packing and unpacking GPU data.

## `std.shader.compute`
Compute-shader utilities.

## `std.shader.debug`
Shader diagnostics and debug visualization.
